# Product Overview

**Synthesized Ether AI** is a SaaS platform for sales automation powered by AI. It deploys "Oliver," a virtual sales consultant that captures and qualifies leads 24/7, schedules consultations directly in Google Calendar, and responds in milliseconds.

## Core Features

- **AI Sales Consultant (Oliver)** — Real-time chat with customizable sales identity, automatic purchase intent detection, and contact capture
- **Google Calendar Integration** — Oliver detects scheduling intent, extracts date/time from chat, and creates calendar events with client invitations
- **Custom Instructions** — Upload `.md` or `.txt` files with sales scripts, pricing, and objection handling; injected into every conversation
- **Document Sandbox** — Upload PDFs and chat with their content using Llama 3
- **Metrics Dashboard** — Interactions, conversion rate, sentiment analysis, and funnel visualization
- **Multi-Model Support** — Groq (Llama 3.3 70B, Mixtral, Gemma 2) and OpenAI (GPT-4o)
- **Premium Design** — Glassmorphism effects, smooth animations, "The Synthesized Ether" design system

## Target Market

Web agencies and businesses in Argentina that need automated lead capture and qualification. Payment processing via Mercado Pago (ARS currency).

## Key Differentiators

- Autonomous 24/7 operation
- Direct calendar integration with automatic scheduling
- Customizable AI personality and sales scripts
- Fast response times (milliseconds)
- Argentina-focused payment integration
